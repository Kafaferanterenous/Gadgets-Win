MOVE REMINDER
-------------
Add an optional reminder to move in the next version of Gadgets.

Proposed behaviour:

* The reminder interval is entered or selected by the user in Settings.
* The predefined/default interval is 60 minutes.
* When the interval expires, temporarily replace the existing "ShutDown"
  label with:

                       -> Move <-

* Display the Move reminder for 30 seconds, then restore the normal
  "ShutDown" label.
* Do not add a separate panel. Fit the reminder controls into the existing
  Settings interface.
* The user can optionally enable a short beep or other suitable reminder
  sound.


POINTS TO DECIDE NEXT TIME
--------------------------
* Sensible minimum and maximum user-entered intervals.
* Whether the interval is entered as minutes, selected from common values,
  or both.
* Whether the reminder timer resets when Gadgets starts, when Settings are
  saved, or after each completed reminder.
* Which Windows sound is noticeable without being harsh or alarming.
* Whether the sound should play once or use a very short two-tone signal.
* Whether the Move label should flash or remain steady. A steady label is
  likely less distracting and should be the initial choice.
* How the reminder behaves while Settings is open or while Windows is
  shutting down.
* Whether changing the ShutDown label affects its click target. Prefer to
  retain the existing control and behaviour unless the user chooses otherwise.


INITIAL DESIGN OPINION
----------------------
This is a good fit for the current compact interface. Reusing the ShutDown
label for 30 seconds makes the reminder prominent without consuming permanent
screen space. A 60-minute default is reasonable, while a user-defined interval
and optional sound allow people to choose how intrusive the reminder should be.

The next implementation should also make it immediately clear in Settings that
the reminder and its sound are optional.
